Meet our lead pastors

Ransome & Rita
Adewale.

Pastor Ransome Adewale is the Founder and Lead Pastor of Charis Tribe Global Church. He is the President of Ransome Adewale Ministries International and founder of the School of Maturity and Ministry.

Through Evolve30, Charis Campmeeting, and Grace Faith Conferences, his ministry establishes people in the knowledge of Jesus Christ and the ministry of the Holy Spirit.

Pastor Rita is his wife, co-lead pastor, and partner in life and ministry. Together, they’re raising a family and serving a growing global church community.
