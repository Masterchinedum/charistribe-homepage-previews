Welcome to Charis Tribe

Charis

/ˈkær.ɪs/ · Greek · noun

Grace — the free, unearned favour of God. The heartbeat of everything we teach.

Charis Tribe is more than a church. We are a family rooted in faith, guided by God’s Word and driven by purpose a vibrant community of young adults committed to apostolic teaching, world evangelism and societal transformation.

Every Sunday 8:00 am

And midweek on Wednesdays at 6:00 PM. Come early — there’s a seat saved for you.

Welcome to Charis Tribe.

Our vision is to raise kingdom‑minded world changers who influence every sphere of life with the gospel. To do this, we teach the undiluted truth of Scripture with Christ as the central focus.

We take discipleship seriously — and organise regular meetings, retreats and seminars focused on achieving our God‑given vision.

(03)Our lead pastors

Pastors Ransome & Rita Adewale

Lead Pastor · Co‑Lead Pastor · Lagos, Nigeria

“Establishing God’s people in the knowledge of Jesus Christ and the ministry of the Holy Spirit.”

Pastor Ransome Adewale is the Founder and Lead Pastor of Charis Tribe Global Church. He ministers with a strong flow of the anointing of the Holy Spirit, accompanied with healings and miracles — and is also a songwriter and an instrumentalist.

Read the full storyShow less

He is the President of Ransome Adewale Ministries International and the founder of the School of Maturity and Ministry — an interdenominational outfit committed to strong discipleship, equipping believers for the work of ministry and the strengthening of local churches.

He champions Evolve30, an online Bible study where he writes daily to help people prioritise spiritual growth and develop a culture of Bible study. He is the convener of Charis Campmeeting — an annual assemblage of believers upholding the finished works of Jesus Christ through sound doctrine and the demonstration of the Spirit and power — and organises Grace Faith Conferences from city to city.

He is married to Pastor Rita, co‑lead Pastor at Charis Tribe Global Church and his partner in life and ministry. They reside in Lagos, Nigeria, and are blessed with two beautiful children, Daystar and Heaven.

(06)Our core values C.H.A.R.I.S

Six letters. One culture.

We are raising a community of world changers globally through C.H.A.R.I.S — a spiritual culture shaped by care, sound doctrine, responsibility and sacrificial love.

  1. 01 C

    Care

    We love people practically. No one walks alone in this house.

  2. 02 H

    Herald

    We proclaim the good news of Jesus Christ — always, everywhere.

  3. 03 A

    Accountability

    We grow in honest relationships that keep us faithful.

  4. 04 R

    Relationship

    With God first, then with one another — real, lasting connection.

  5. 05 I

    Indoctrination

    Grounded in sound doctrine and the finished work of Christ.

  6. 06 S

    Sacrifice

    We give our best — time, talent and treasure — for the gospel.
