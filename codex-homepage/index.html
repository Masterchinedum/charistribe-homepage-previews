Your first Sunday starts here

Come as you are.
Leave as family.

No perfect people. No dress code. Just a warm welcome, a seat for you, and the good news of Jesus.

We’d love to meet you
What can I expect on my first visit?

Expect heartfelt worship, teaching from the Bible, and a community that’s glad you came. Our welcome team will help you settle in and find your way around.

What should I wear?

Come in whatever makes you feel comfortable. Whether casual or dressed up, you’ll be warmly welcomed.

Can I bring my children?

Absolutely. Families are welcome at Charis Tribe. Ask the welcome team about children’s ministry when you arrive, or contact us beforehand to learn what’s available at your campus.

Can I be part of the church online?

Yes. Join our services and explore teachings on our YouTube channel. Wherever you are, you can grow in faith with the Charis Tribe family. Visit our channel ↗
